Oscar Medina is a proud graduate of Dwight D. Eisenhower High School, Class of 2000. Since 2019, he has been a dedicated educator on our campus and was honored as Teacher of the Year for the 2023–2024 school year. Mr. Medina sponsors the National Art Honor Society alongside Ms. Kayleigh Rundquist, providing students opportunities to serve the community. He also supports Esports alongside Mr. Moises Casco on the Senior Campus and Mr. Jose Muniz on the 9th Grade Campus. His commitment to education, creativity, and student opportunities continues to make a lasting impact on our campus community.
